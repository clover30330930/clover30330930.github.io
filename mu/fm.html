when i went to japan, my host family took me to see tokenainamae live, because i'd told them it was my favorite Japanese band, and there happened to be a show in Shimokitazawa, Tokyo overlapping with my stay.

Shimokitazawa itself is a mixed bag. it's the grungy-music prefecture of tokyo. my host-father is a huge anime fan and bocchi the rock takes place here, so he wanted to take me to all the spots and recreate screenshots from the show. XD
the main streets are gentrified with retro Americana style fashion. it's v classic rock, leather biker jackets, sourced 90s sportswear. it's not really my thing. there are a few off-beaten paths with cute little vintage shops that are a little more grungy and japanesse. there's one tiny lolita shop in a residential area with beautiful handmade dresses and blouses. we shopped and my host family treated me to pancakes before da show ^__^

it was an underground basement venue, through a narrow staircase you might miss if you were just walking by... there were probably 30-40 people there total with the bands included. the crowd was pretty much entirely men in their late 20s and early 30s, lots of white-collar salarymen. i mightve been the only girl in the audience? if this band played in the west, i imagine it'd be a predominately female audience, so this surprised me. everyone was so respectful and still LOL. i just respectfully nodded my head and swayed to the music, my host-father started getting into it, too.

afterwards, we waited in line for the merch table. my host-father introduced me to the guitarist as an american exchange student and asked them to sign my CD. he reached out his hand for a shake and said slowly, ”あの....hello, it is nice to meet you." he seemed nervous to speak english. he took my CD and came back a minute later with 4 signatures. it was so goated...

my host family was so warm and hospitable and i cant believe they took me to such a legendary show. truly im so lucky ! i was trying not to fall asleep on the train ride home, but kept doing that nodding off and then startling myself awake thing which was embarrassing LOL.
